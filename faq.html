Frequently Asked Questions

Click on a question to see the answer.

What are your opening hours?

+

We are open every day from 8:00 to 22:00, including weekends and holidays.

Do you have plant-based milk?

+

Yes, we have oat, almond and coconut milk. You can choose any of them for no extra cost.

Can I book a table in advance?

+

Yes, you can book a table on our Reservation page. We will confirm your booking by email.

Do you sell coffee beans to take home?

+

Yes, we sell freshly roasted coffee beans in 250 g and 1 kg packs. We can also grind them for you.

Is there free Wi-Fi in the coffee shop?

+

Yes, we have free Wi-Fi for all our guests. Ask the barista for the password.

What do members get after signing in?

+

Members get every 10th coffee for free and special offers on new drinks.
